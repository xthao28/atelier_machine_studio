# Usage guidelines

## Content hierarchy

1. The headline or one dominant graphic reads first.
2. Technical identity (project number, experiment ID, tags) reads second.
3. Body text and annotations read last. If a slide needs more than 25 words, split it.

## Do

- Use real hardware photographs and the supplied XUNA reference art.
- Let negative space do the work; leave at least a third of a slide quiet.
- Pair every status colour with a shape and a word.
- Keep one accent field per slide and one filled tag.
- Check each headline in Vietnamese before publishing a bilingual post.

## Don't

- Don't use gradients, glow, shadows, neon or glitch effects.
- Don't set paragraphs in mono or decorative fonts; don't go below the minimum sizes.
- Don't place text on halftone, grain-heavy areas or the photo itself without a `paper` or `ink` plate.
- Don't redraw XUNA, don't invent a logo mark, don't add stock or generated robot imagery.
- Don't add a graphic element without a job from the Graphic language list.

## Image treatment

- Photos: natural colour, shot square-on or at a clear three-quarter angle, on a plain bench. Place them in `ImageFrame` with the halftone fade at the edge and a mono caption bar.
- Pixel art: scale by whole multiples, nearest-neighbour, on a flat field with the dot grid at most.
- Diagrams: lines at 3 px, nodes in `lime`, `lilac`, `orange`, labels in `label`.

## Consistency checklist

- Header and footer on every slide; same positions on every post.
- Colours only from the tokens; type only from the styles.
- Light and dark versions both checked at phone size.
