A framed rectangle for real hardware photography with crosshair corners and a mono caption bar.

- Photos are real: bench, breadboard, printed parts. Never use stock or generated robot imagery.
- Treat the photo with a light `paper-shade` ground and the halftone fade; do not apply filters or colour casts.
- Caption bar: figure number left, view or scale right. Crosshairs sit 24 px outside the frame corners.
