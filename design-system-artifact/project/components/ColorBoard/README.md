The five official colours, their intended share of a layout and the measured text contrast of every approved pair.

- Paper 45 · Ink 30 · Lime 10 · Lilac 10 · Orange 5 is a guideline; no post has to contain all five.
- Never introduce a new dominant colour. Tints are limited to `paper-shade`, `ink-soft` and `paper-soft`.
- Approved text pairs: `ink` on `paper`, `lime`, `lilac`, `orange`; `paper` and `lime` on `ink`. Do not set `orange` or `lilac` text on `paper`.
